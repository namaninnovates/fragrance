Raw Materials

We source only the rarest botanicals from around the world. ONIRIQUE relies on the extreme depth of aged Laotian agarwood and fossilized Baltic amber.

Agarwood (Oud)

Ages for a minimum of 9 years to develop its complex, animalic, and deeply woody profile.

Golden Amber

A resinous, warm, and sweet baseline that acts as the foundation of this midnight composition.

Layering Suggestion

NEIGE

Brighten the heavy, ancient oud base of ONIRIQUE by layering it with the radiant white florals and neroli of NEIGE. The result is a striking contrast of light and dark.
